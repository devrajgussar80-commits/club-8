Withdrawal pop-up

The first pop-up on the withdrawal screen. Shown when a player's wallet is below the smallest withdrawal you will process — they need a bigger balance, not a recharge, so its button sends them to the games rather than the deposit screen.

Write {minimum} where the smallest withdrawal should appear and {balance} for what the player currently holds. This is the same figure as “Minimum withdrawal” on the Payments tab — changing it in either place changes both.

Deposit pop-up

The second pop-up, shown only once a player's wallet has cleared the minimum above. They cannot withdraw until their approved recharges reach this total; anyone who tries is shown the message below, with a button that takes them to the recharge screen.

Write {minimum} where the required amount should appear and {deposited} for what they have recharged so far. Set the amount to 0 to switch the lock off.

    Signup bonus run

    What a new account is given, and how far it can ride on it. Until a player's wallet reaches the target drawn for them, this share of their single-player rounds — Lucky Reels, Mega Slots, Roulette, Mines, Chicken Road — is won. The target is drawn once per account, somewhere in the band, and the boost never carries anyone past the top of it. Shared-round games are untouched: WinGo, Dice, Fish vs Tiger, Vortex and XAviator deal one result to the whole table.

    An open run also unlocks the arcade without a recharge — a ₹100 wallet could never clear the deposit threshold on its own. Once the run ends, the usual deposit gate is back.
